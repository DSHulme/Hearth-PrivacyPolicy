Privacy Policy

Hearth · Effective 6 November 2026

In short: Hearth does not collect your name, account or any personal information, and it does not run any servers that store your data. Everything you create in the app stays on your own device. The only things that ever leave your phone are a single word when you ask for a meaning the app does not already have, and an optional tip handled by Apple.

Who we are

Hearth is a Bible reading app made by [YOUR NAME] ("we", "us"), based in England, United Kingdom. You can contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL].

What stays on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so that it works the way you left it:

  • Your highlights, saved verses and notes
  • Your prayer list, memory verses, and reading plan or devotion progress
  • Your settings, such as theme, colours, font, text size, reminder time and reading position
  • Words you add to My Words

This information is never sent to us or to any server we run. We do not have accounts, logins or a back end, so we cannot see it.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or location
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising identifiers or advertising
  • No crash reports sent to us
  • No data shared with or sold to third parties

Word meanings and the internet

The Bible text, stories and many simple word meanings are stored inside the app, which works without an internet connection. When you tap a word and the app does not have its meaning stored, it can look the word up online. To do this the app sends only that single word to two free services:

  • The Free Dictionary API (dictionaryapi.dev), for definitions
  • Datamuse (datamuse.com), for similar words

As with any web request, these services can see your device's internet address (IP address) and the kind of device you use. The app sends no name, account or other detail about you, and we receive nothing from these services. Each service has its own privacy policy. If you are offline, the app tells you and carries on with the meanings it already has.

Features that use your device

  • Reminders. If you turn on daily reminders, the app asks permission to send notifications. They are scheduled on your device and do not involve any server. You can turn them off in the app or in your phone's Settings.
  • Read aloud. The app uses the speech voices built into your phone. The text is read out on your device.
  • Share as a picture and Back up. When you share a verse or export your notes, the app hands the picture or text to your phone's share sheet. Where it goes is your choice, and the app you send it to then handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • Keep screen awake. This only stops the screen from locking while you read or listen.

Apple

Apple may collect its own information about downloads and, if you have chosen to share them, app usage and crash data. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy, and we receive only anonymous summaries, if any, through App Store Connect.

Tips and payment

Hearth is free to download and every feature is free. The only thing you can buy is an optional tip, which unlocks nothing. Tips are bought through the Apple App Store. Apple handles the whole purchase, including your payment details and Apple ID. We never see or store them, and we receive only anonymous sales totals from Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple under its own terms. Tips are processed with the help of a service called RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous purchase identifier and no name or contact details. The app has no advertising.

Children

Hearth is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect any from children.

Your rights and deleting your data

Because your information stays on your device, you are in full control of it. You can delete individual items inside the app, or delete all of it by deleting the app from your phone. If you have exported a backup or shared a picture, delete those copies where you saved them.

If you are in the UK or EU, you have rights under data protection law, including access, correction and deletion. As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or erase, but you are welcome to contact us at [SUPPORT EMAIL] with any question. You may also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above. We will only make a change that adds data collection with a clear notice in the app.
